Privacy Policy for Shop Management App

Effective Date: September 27, 2026

My Small Shop ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Shop Management App mobile application ("Service"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.

1. Information Collection and Use

We collect information required to provide inventory and shop management services:

  • Account Data: Email address, name, and user role for account login and access control.
  • Business Data: Inventory items, stock counts, sales, purchases, and supplier records entered for your shop.
  • Diagnostics: Crash logs via Firebase Crashlytics to maintain application stability.

2. Data Security & Third-Party Services

We use secure Google Firebase services to encrypt and store data:

  • Firebase Authentication (Secure user sign-in)
  • Cloud Firestore & Storage (Encrypted database sync and photo storage)
  • Google Play Services

3. Data Deletion Rights

You can request account or shop data deletion at any time by contacting us at mysmallshop.support@gmail.com.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us by email: mysmallshop.support@gmail.com
