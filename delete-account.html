Account & Data Deletion Request

App: Shop Management App

Developer: My Small Shop

If you wish to delete your account and associated data from the Shop Management App, please follow the steps below.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion:

  1. Send an email to: mysmallshop.support@gmail.com
  2. Subject: Account Deletion Request - [Your Registered Email]
  3. Include your registered email address and shop name in the body of the email.

Data That Will Be Deleted:

  • Account Profile: Your email address, display name, and authentication credentials.
  • Shop Records: Inventory items, sales history, purchase records, and supplier listings.
  • Uploaded Media: Product images uploaded to Cloud Storage.

Data Retention Period:

All account profile records and shop data will be permanently deleted from Firebase servers within 7 business days of receiving your request.
